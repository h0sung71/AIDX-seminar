### Carbon deposits
Hydrocarbon structures
 - Paraffins : rapid evaporation, froms soot, least propensity to form liquid deposits (BEST)
 - Olefins & Cyclic HC : less rapid evaporation, form soot copiously, higher propensity to form liquid deposits (NOT GOOD)
 - Aromatics : slowest evaporation, form soot and liquid deposits (BAD)
 - Sulphur : causes liquid pyrolysis and oxidation reactions (TOO BAD)

### Ignition
 Ignition systems
  - surface discharge igniter : aircraft
  - torch igniter : ground based
  - flow plug, hot surface ignition : safety
  - plasma jet : afterburner
  - laser ignition : automotive
  - Ignition energy depends on the equivalence ratio, drop size, fuel type
  - Igniter should be placed inside the recirculation zone and not after the cooling slot.
  - Igniter should be not directly hitted by fuel injection; fuel should be enough position in the "spreading" zone
 Ignition phases
  1) initial flame kernel
  2) flame kernel spreads out
  3) reaction survives in whole combustor region
 Autoignition
  - Ignition time correlation is calculated based on Arrhenius equation
  - Li & Williams (recommended!)
  - Fuel effects is considerable; especially ethalon brings a quite big difference
  - Until now, experimental result and theroetical prediction deviates in high temperature regime

### Reducing NOx
 Decrease peak temperature
  - injecting water or steam diluent
  - Direct lean burn (DLN)
 Remove NOx from exhaust stream
  - catalytic reduction (CSR)
 Decrease **activiation energy**
  Catalyst combustion
     fully catalytic : simple strategy, but high temperature difference b/w surface relative cool gas
     fuel staged : injecting fuel remainder after catalyst
     air staged : injecting air remainder after catalyst
     staged catalytic : combined lean + homogenous
  - CH4 oxidation promoted by nobel metals ex) platinum, palladium oxide (PdO)
  - Constraints
     Inlet gas temperature high enough to initiate catalytic reactions
     Outlet gas temperature high enough to initate homogenous combustion and complete combustion
     Catalyst wall is limited to protect catalyst substances
  - **Kawasaki** gas turbine "CATALYTICA XONON Technology" or "K-lean"
  - It ends up at 2018, no longer lists GPB-15x
  Surface stabilized combustion technology
   - minimize resideance time to reduce NOx
  Thermal oxidizer combustion technology
   - Low temperature, long time to convert chemical energy into heat
