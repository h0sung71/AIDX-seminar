Laminar Round jet (Rayleigh mechanism)
 - any disturbance on a jet with wavelength greater than the jet circumference will grow (wavelength > pi diameter)
 - growth rate q_max = 0.97 (surface_tension^1 density^-1 diameter^-3)^0.5
 - critical wavelength (corresponding to the fastest growing disturbance) ~ 4.51 d
 - The diameter of oscillating jet ~ 1.89 d
 - Spherical droplet (after breaks) ~ 1.89 d
 - Reyleigh timescale for breakup ~ sqrt(density^1 diameter^3 surface tension^-1)
 - viscocapillary timescale ~ diamter^1 surface tension^-1
 - Air friction reduces the critical wavelength ~ 2.8 d at 15 m/s

Rapid breakup (Prompt)
 - Maximum relative velocity or most normal angle of incidence
 - Drop size depend less upon properties of air/liquid
 - break up in crossflow
 - Atomization performance has **no viscosity effect, no air pressure effect** in HIGH AIR MOMNETUM (ex air-blast)

**Flow number (FN)**
 - FN = mass / sqrt (pressure_difference)
     or mass / sqrt (density pressure_difference)

Evaporation
 Single drop evaporation
  - m = drop mass = pi/6 density diameter^3 
  - evaporation constant (lambda) = d(diameter ^2) / dt
 Drop lifetime : D-squared Law
  - D0^2 - D^ = lambda time
 Heat up time : the initial slop is less than the slop after heat up
  - Two evaporation constants, but they become similar when a droplet is exposed to high temperature
  - Pressure dependency with high temperature
 Convection effect : droplet exposed to hot air
 burning rate : droplet exposed to hot flame

 Multi drop evaporation (spray)
  - m = drop mass = n pi/6 density diameter^3 
  - Use fuel_air_ratio (FAR), q
  - m = density evaporation_ratio volume_air FAR / diameter^2 
  - higher q -> higher MMD
  - Follows same principle : t = D^2/evaporation_ratio
